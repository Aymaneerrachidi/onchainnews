        How a coin gets in

        Two doors

        Created inside the last 24 hours and up at least 30%, or any age doing a 5x or better. Floors of $150k market cap and $50k daily volume apply to both.

        Risk is shown, not hidden

        Failing one check does not remove a coin. Concentration, thin pools, paid boosts, recycled tickers and fades are printed on the row so the judgement stays yours.

        Only rugs are removed

        A live mint or freeze authority, unlocked liquidity, bundled supply or a manufactured tape. Those are listed separately rather than silently dropped.
